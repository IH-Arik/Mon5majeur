# Mon5Majeur — QA Summary Report

*Manual test session — annotated screenshots — 08/08/2026*

## Summary

Issues identified during a manual test session across the app (Home, Shop, Global League, Profile, Customization, Private League creation screens). Two recurring root causes stand out:

- **Untranslated text or broken i18n keys** (item 1) — likely an incomplete translation pass on the studio side.
- **Parity gaps between Global League and Private League** (items 4, 5) — features built for the private league were not replicated on the global league screen.
- **Wrong asset pool in use** (items 7, 8) — incorrect or incomplete ID/asset mapping on the customization screens.

---

## 1. Home — Untranslated / broken text when empty

**Priority: Medium**

Final validated copy to integrate:

> No games tonight 🏀

> No league yet... come challenge your friends 👊

**Action:** replace the broken/English i18n keys (`noMatchesToday`, `No leagues joined yet`) with this FR copy, and provide the EN equivalent above. Fix the i18n binding itself, not just the displayed text.

---

## 2. Shop — Token top-up page unreachable

**Priority: High**

There is no way to reach a token top-up screen from the Shop. The balance shown at the top (6 tokens) has no entry point to get more.

**Action:** check with the studio why the top-up page isn't accessible/visible right now, and confirm it will be in place before launch.

---

## 3. Shop — Live Scoring: text and price out of sync

**Priority: Medium**

The live score feature was modified a while back because it was running too far behind — ad removal was set aside at that point, and is therefore no longer part of the offer for now. The Shop copy ("Live - Scoring + No Ads" / "Real-time & ad-free") and the displayed price (450 tokens/Year) still reflect the old model, from before that change.

**Action:** update the copy to "Live Scoring" / "Follow your players in real time" (no ad mention), and correct the price to reflect the new model (200 tokens/month).

---

## 4. Global League — "Bonuses" button shown by mistake

**Priority: High**

The Global League runs on the no-bonus duel engine (unlike private leagues: Luxury Tax, Chef Curry, 6th Man). The "+ Bonuses" button still shows up on the team composition screen in the Global League.

**Action:** hide the button conditionally on league type. Also verify server-side that applying a bonus is rejected in the Global League, even if the button remains clickable by mistake.

---

## 5. Global League — "Team confirmed" badge missing

**Priority: High**

As validated in the Figma v0, a green confirmation badge should appear once the team is complete and validated. It is implemented and working in the Private League, but missing in the Global League during testing.

**Action:** replicate the visual state on the Global League screen. Run a full parity audit between both composition screens (confirmed, locked, over budget, bonus, etc.) rather than fixing issues one by one.

---

## 7. Customization — Jersey selection: missing designs

**Priority: High**

6 jersey designs exist in total: DEVIL #45, FLOWER #21, UFO #97, SHARK #72, SNAKE #45 (missing in-app), ZEBRA #19 (missing in-app).

Yet the in-app screen only shows 5 tiles total, with the FLOWER #21 jersey duplicated in slots 2 and 5.

**Result:** the SNAKE and ZEBRA designs are never reachable by players — they don't appear anywhere in the current picker.

**Action:** the selection grid only has 5 slots when it needs 6 — add a 6th slot to the grid, then fix the ID → asset mapping so each of the 6 designs (DEVIL, FLOWER, UFO, SHARK, SNAKE, ZEBRA) is unique and properly referenced, with no duplicates.
